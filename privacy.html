Who is responsible

Mouge Perfume is responsible for the invitation-list data. For privacy requests, questions, or to withdraw marketing consent, contact mougeperfumes@gmail.com.

What we collect and why

If you request an invitation, we collect your email address, your affirmative marketing-consent choice, the consent-text version, and a submission timestamp. We use these details to manage the launch invitation list and send launch/marketing emails only after you opt in. Signing up is optional; you can browse the site without submitting any data.

Service providers and transfers

The form sends your submission to FormSubmit so it can deliver it to our inbox. FormSubmit's documentation says form submissions are retained for 30 days. Its help page says its spam prevention uses Google reCAPTCHA v2 and v3, which may process technical information; see Google's privacy policy. FormSubmit's publicly available privacy notice does not specify the country where form data is processed. This service may involve processing outside Egypt. Please review FormSubmit's privacy notice. We also use Cloudflare Pages to deliver and protect the website; Cloudflare may process technical request information under its privacy policy.

Retention

Your address remains on the active invitation list until the launch campaign ends or you withdraw consent, whichever happens first. We retain evidence of consent or withdrawal for three years after the last marketing message. The FormSubmit service separately states that it retains form submissions for 30 days; copies delivered to our inbox are handled under this notice and our account settings.

Your choices and requests

You may withdraw consent at any time by emailing mougeperfumes@gmail.com with “Unsubscribe” in the subject. You may also request access, correction, or deletion, subject to records we are required to retain. We do not sell your email address or use it for unrelated purposes.

Cookies and analytics

Mouge Perfume does not add visitor-analytics or advertising-tracking code in this release. The hosting and form providers may process technical information as described in their own terms.
